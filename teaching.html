Ubiqitous Movement Sensing

Course info

In December 2024, Tallinn University hosted the Erasmus BIP (Blended Intensive Programme) titled Ubiquitous Movement Sensing, offering students a unique opportunity to dive into the world of embodied sensing and movement analysis. Over the course of five days, from December 16 to 20, students from across Europe came together to engage in an intensive course that combined theory, practice, and hands-on experience in the fascinating field of movement tracking.
